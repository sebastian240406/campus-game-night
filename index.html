Campus Game Night

About the Event

Campus Game Night brings college students together for board games, video games, and friendly competition. Students can come with friends or join a table and meet someone new. The event is for beginners and experienced players who want a fun break from classes.

Event Activities

Students can play card games, board games, and friendly video game challenges.

  • Welcome and find a table
  • Open play and friendly challenges
  • Final round and wrap-up
